# Ice Bath and Sauna Prices in Dubai: What You Pay and What's Included

If you're looking for an ice bath or infrared sauna in Dubai, prices can be hard to compare. Some places charge for a day pass, some only offer memberships, and some don't list prices at all. Here's exactly what a session at Beit Sisu costs, what's included, and how to choose the right option for you.

## How much is an ice bath and sauna session in Dubai at Beit Sisu?

A private contrast therapy session (infrared sauna, cold plunge and red light therapy in your own suite) costs:

- **1 person:** AED 160 for 30 minutes, AED 220 for 60 minutes
- **2 people:** AED 200 for 30 minutes, AED 260 for 60 minutes

Every session includes all three: you don't pay extra for the cold plunge or the red light.

## How much is a cold plunge only?

If you just want the ice bath, you can book a private cold plunge room:

- **1 person:** AED 85 for 15 minutes, AED 120 for 30 minutes
- **2 people:** AED 160 for 30 minutes (the room has one barrel, so you take turns)

## Is there an offer for first-time visitors?

Yes. The **Intro Offer** gives you **3 × 60-minute contrast therapy sessions for AED 350**. That works out at under AED 117 a session. It's for one person and valid for three months.

## How much is a monthly membership?

If you come regularly, a membership is the best value:

- **Silver:** 4 × 60-minute sessions a month for AED 599
- **Gold:** 8 × 60-minute sessions a month for AED 1,149
- **Black:** unlimited 60-minute sessions a month, and you can bring a friend for free, for AED 1,899

Members book their sessions through the website or on WhatsApp.

## What's included in the price?

Every session is in a completely private suite, so nobody else shares your room. Included:

- a full-spectrum **[infrared sauna](/services/infrared-saunas/)**, 50–79°C, which fits two people
- a **[cold plunge](/services/cold-plunges/)** kept between 3.5 and 8°C, with water filtered around the clock
- a **[red light therapy](/services/red-light-therapy/)** tower
- your own shower, a towel and Gymskin shower products
- free filtered water, and a Bluetooth speaker for your own music
- **free valet parking** at the Arjaan by Rotana, Dubai Media City

All you need to bring is a swimsuit.

## Should I book 30 or 60 minutes?

- **30 minutes** is enough for two or three rounds of hot and cold, and it's great before work or on a busy day.
- **60 minutes** gives you time for three or four full rounds (about 10 minutes in the sauna, then 30 seconds to a minute in the plunge), plus time to shower and relax. Most first-timers enjoy the full hour.

New to it? Our guide [What is contrast therapy?](/what-is-contrast-therapy/) explains how a session works.

## Can I rent an ice bath for home or an event?

Yes. We rent **portable ice baths**, delivered, set up and collected anywhere in Dubai, with a chiller or ice. Prices depend on the length of the rental and the location, so [message us on WhatsApp](https://wa.me/971585963805) for a quote. See [Ice Bath Rental Dubai](/ice-bath-rental-dubai/).

## Do you offer group or corporate rates?

Yes. We offer **corporate memberships** for companies, and we work with running clubs, golf societies and social groups, creating a dedicated discount code for your members. Get in touch through our [Collaborations page](/event-partnership-enquiries/).

## Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes. Beit Sisu gift vouchers make a great present for anyone who loves to recover and relax. Email [hello@beitsisu.com](mailto:hello@beitsisu.com) to organise one.

## Can I cancel or reschedule?

Please let us know at least **12 hours before** your session. If the studio is quiet, we can often help at shorter notice.
