>
> Every Beit Sisu contrast therapy suite includes a red light therapy tower, so you can add it to your sauna and cold plunge session at no extra cost.

> **What is red light therapy?**
> Red light therapy uses panels of red and near-infrared light that work at the level of your cells to support healing and recovery in skin, hair and muscles. It's different from an infrared sauna, which uses heat to raise your body temperature and make you sweat. Using both in one session gives you the benefits of each.
>
> **What people use it for**
> - **Skin:** softening fine lines, calming breakouts and helping scars heal.

>
> **How to use it in your session**
> Stand or sit facing the tower during your session (between sauna rounds works well). There's nothing to prepare and nothing to wear except your swimsuit.
>

> Included in every contrast therapy session: 1 person from AED 160, 2 people from AED 200, or the Intro Offer (3 × 60 min) for AED 350.

Recommendation:
- Use **one** tool only: **LiteSpeed Cache**, because your server runs LiteSpeed.
- Exclude `/book-appointment/`, `/thank-you/`, `/my-bookings/`, the cancel/reschedule pages and `/bookingpress-complete-payment/` from caching.
