# Notes for App Review

Paste into App Store Connect → version page → **App Review Information**. Before submitting,
create the demo account on the live site and fill in its details below.

**Sign-in required:** Yes

| | |
|---|---|
| Username | `review@[your domain]` (create it on the live site: Sign up → with email) |
| Password | `[a strong password]` |

**Notes** (English is fine for review):

> ReKit is a free marketplace for second-hand sports equipment in the UAE. Users post ads,
> browse by sport and emirate, and chat with buyers and sellers.
>
> **Logging in:** please use the demo account above. Tap Account → "Use email and password
> instead". (Normal users log in with a UAE mobile number and an SMS code.)
>
> **Native features in the app:**
> - Push notifications through APNs when someone sends you a message. To test: Account →
>   "Turn on notifications", then reply from the website in a browser as a second account,
>   or ask us and we'll send a message.
> - The native share sheet on every ad (Share button).
> - Universal links: rekit.ae links open in the app.
> - Camera and photo library access when adding photos to an ad.
>
> **User-generated content** (Guideline 1.2):
> - Ads for banned items are filtered when posted (weapons, drugs and steroids, counterfeits, live animals).
> - Every ad has "Report this ad".
> - Every chat has "Block", which can also report the chat. Blocked users can't message you.
> - Our team reviews reports within 24 hours.
> - Terms of Use: https://rekit.ae/terms. Contact: https://rekit.ae/help.
>
> **Account deletion** (Guideline 5.1.1(v)): Account → "Delete your account".
>
> **Payments:** the app has no in-app purchases. Buying and selling between users is arranged
> by the users and paid in person, outside the app. Businesses can buy advertising only on our
> website, and the app shows no purchase buttons or links for it.

**Contact information:** your name, phone and email for Apple to reach you during review.

## Age rating questionnaire

Answer honestly. The likely answers are:

- **User-generated content:** Yes
- **Messaging and chat:** Yes
- **Advertising:** Yes
- No violence, sexual content, gambling, contests, medical information, or unrestricted web access (other links open in Safari)

Then set the rating to **18+** to match the Terms of Use, which require users to be 18 or over.
