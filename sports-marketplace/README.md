# ReKit: second-hand sports equipment marketplace

"ReKit" is a placeholder name. A free classifieds marketplace for used sports gear,
launching in the UAE with the rest of the GCC to follow. Users never pay;
advertisers pay for placements.

| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`web/`](web/README.md) | **The real app.** SMS login, English + Arabic, listings with photos, chat, saved items, reports, ad slots, admin. Start here. |
| [`prototype/`](prototype/index.html) | The first clickable mock-up (one HTML file, sample data, no backend). |

## Revenue model (ads only, users always free)

1. **Homepage banner:** a flat weekly fee, one advertiser at a time.
2. **Sport takeover:** banner and in-feed ads inside one sport, e.g. a padel shop showing only to padel buyers.
3. **Sponsored listings for businesses:** shops pay to put their stock at the top of matching searches. Private sellers never pay.
4. **Listing-page display ads (CPM):** targeted by sport and emirate.
5. **Newsletter features:** a weekly "fresh gear" email.
6. **Event and club partnerships:** races, tournaments, academies.

Ad rules: every ad is labelled, only sport, fitness and outdoor advertisers, no pop-ups or autoplay.
