How a session works

Arrive, go quiet, alternate.

  1. 01 · Arrive

    Free valet

    Drive up to the Arjaan by Rotana in Dubai Media City. Valet parking is free.

  2. 02 · Your suite

    Yours alone

    A private room for you, or you and a friend. Put your own playlist on the speaker.

  3. 03 · Heat ↔ cold

    Rounds

    About 10 minutes in the sauna, 30–60 seconds in the plunge. Repeat 3–4 times.

  4. 04 · 30 or 60 min

    Shower and go

    Your own shower, a towel and Gymskin products. Just bring a swimsuit.
