How a session works

Arrive, go quiet, alternate.

  1. 01 · Arrive

    Free valet

    Drive up to the Arjaan by Rotana. Valet is free.

  2. 02 · Your suite

    Yours alone

    Just you, or you and a friend. Your own playlist.

  3. 03 · Heat ↔ cold

    Rounds

    ~10 min sauna, 30–60 sec plunge. Repeat 3–4 times.

  4. 04 · 30 or 60 min

    Shower and go

    Own shower, towel, Gymskin. Bring a swimsuit.
