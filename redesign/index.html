How a session works

Sixty minutes,
accounted for.

First time? We'll show you around the suite and give you a few pointers. After that, the room is yours.

  1. 00:00

    Arrive

    Free valet at the hotel lobby. We walk you to your private suite, you connect your phone to the speaker and close the door.

  2. 00:05

    Heat

    About 10 minutes in the full-spectrum infrared sauna at 50–79°C, with the red light tower beside you.

  3. 00:15

    Cold

    30 seconds to a minute in the cedar cold plunge at 3.5–8°C. Stay a little longer each round.

  4. ×3–4

    Repeat

    Three to four hot-cold cycles in an hour is our recommendation. Go at your own pace.

  5. 00:50

    Shower & go

    Your own shower, right in the suite. Dressed, out and back to your day, reset.

Only have 30 minutes? Do one or two rounds. Just want the cold? Book a 15-minute plunge.
