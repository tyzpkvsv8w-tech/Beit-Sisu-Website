# Looking for a Sauna in Dubai? How to Choose the Right One

Dubai has more saunas than ever: in gyms, hotel spas, wellness clubs and private studios. But they're not all the same experience. If you're searching for a sauna in Dubai, here's a simple guide to what's out there and what to look for, so you end up somewhere you'll actually want to go back to.

## The main types of sauna in Dubai

**Gym saunas.** Many gyms have a sauna in the changing rooms. They're convenient if you already train there, but they're usually shared, busy at peak times, and there's rarely a cold plunge nearby.

**Hotel and spa saunas.** Often beautifully designed and part of a wider spa visit. Great for a treat, but access can mean a day pass, a treatment booking or a hotel stay, and you'll usually share the space with other guests.

**Private sauna suites.** A newer option: you book a suite just for you (or you and a friend) for a set time. Nobody else is in the room, and the best ones pair the sauna with a cold plunge so you can do proper contrast therapy.

## Infrared or traditional?

Most private studios in Dubai use **infrared saunas**. A traditional Finnish sauna heats the air, often to around 80–100°C. An infrared sauna warms your body more directly, so it feels gentler at a lower air temperature, and many people find it easier to relax and stay in longer. We explain the difference in detail in [Infrared Sauna vs Traditional Sauna](/infrared-sauna-vs-traditional-sauna/).

## What to look for

- **Privacy:** do you want to switch off on your own or with a friend, or don't you mind sharing?
- **Cleanliness:** look for places that clean between every booking and give you your own shower and towel.
- **A cold plunge:** sauna on its own is great; sauna plus cold plunge is even better. Alternating the two is called [contrast therapy](/what-is-contrast-therapy/).
- **Temperature control:** being able to ask for a cooler or hotter session makes a big difference, especially for first-timers.
- **Easy booking:** online booking with clear prices and short sessions (30 or 60 minutes) fits around a busy day.
- **Location and parking:** in Dubai, easy parking matters more than you'd think.

## How much does a sauna cost in Dubai?

Prices vary widely. Gym saunas come with a membership, and spa access is often bundled with day passes or treatments. Private sauna suites are usually priced per session. At Beit Sisu, a private session including the infrared sauna, cold plunge and red light therapy starts from AED 160 for 30 minutes.

## Before you go

Saunas aren't suitable for everyone. Please check with your doctor first if you're pregnant, have a heart condition, high blood pressure or any chronic health condition. Drink plenty of water before and after, and never use a sauna after drinking alcohol.
