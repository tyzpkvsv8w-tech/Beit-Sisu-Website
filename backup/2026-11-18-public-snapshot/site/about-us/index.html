    Meet the Owner

    For 12 years, Jack worked on board yachts, climbing the ranks one season at a time. It’s a life that takes you everywhere, and wherever the boat docked, Jack was already looking up the nearest sauna or ice bath. Rain or shine, new country or old favourite, it became a non-negotiable part of how he stayed sharp, steady, and himself.

    When he eventually came ashore in Dubai and started working as a yacht broker, that ritual didn’t fade; if anything, it mattered more. Infrared sauna and ice bath weren’t a holiday indulgence anymore; they were part of his identity, and he wanted them to stay that way.

    Jack is also an avid cyclist, and recovery has always been a huge part of his training, long before it became part of his business. Infrared sauna and ice bath aren’t an occasional treat for him; they’re built into how he trains, recovers, and keeps showing up.

    That’s what led him to Beit Sisu. Jack wanted to build a space that made infrared saunas and ice baths properly accessible and not intimidating or exclusive, just a genuinely friendly environment where anyone could walk in, whether they’re a total beginner or a die-hard regular, and get the real benefits of the practice.

    He’s seen firsthand what a real difference infrared sauna and ice bath make when they become a consistent habit rather than a one-off. Physically and mentally. That’s still the part he loves most: watching people who commit to it change, visibly, for the better.

    Under Jack’s ownership, Beit Sisu carries that same mission forward—a dedicated space for you to reset, refocus, and restart.
