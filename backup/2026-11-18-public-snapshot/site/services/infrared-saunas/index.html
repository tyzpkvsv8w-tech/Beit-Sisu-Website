    Private infrared sauna in Dubai Media City

    At Beit Sisu you never share a sauna with strangers. Each of our contrast therapy suites at the Arjaan by Rotana Hotel in Dubai Media City has its own full-spectrum infrared sauna, a cold plunge, a red light therapy tower and a private shower – all yours for the whole session.

    What a session looks like

    Our infrared saunas run between 50 and 79°C. That’s gentler than a traditional sauna, because infrared warms your body directly rather than heating the air around you. We keep each sauna at its maximum temperature during the day, but just ask if you’d prefer it lower – the benefits still come at any temperature. Sessions are 30 or 60 minutes (if the room is free, you can extend to 90 minutes at the studio), and the sauna fits two people if you’d like to bring a friend.

    Most guests combine heat and cold: about 10 minutes in the sauna, 30 seconds to a minute in the plunge, and repeat 3–4 times in an hour.

    Why people come back

    • Relaxation and better sleep – a session helps your body switch into its “rest” mode, and many guests sleep noticeably better afterwards.
    • Muscle recovery – heat increases blood flow to tired muscles and helps ease inflammation.
    • Skin – sweating boosts circulation and leaves skin looking clearer and brighter.
    • Stress relief – a quiet, private space to switch off from the city.

    What to bring

    Just a swimsuit and water. We provide a towel, Gymskin shower products and filtered water, and you can watch Netflix or play music through the Bluetooth speaker.
