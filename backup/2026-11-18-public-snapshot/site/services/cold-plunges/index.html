    Private cold plunge and ice bath in Dubai

    Looking for an ice bath in Dubai without the crowds? Beit Sisu’s cold plunges are completely private. Book a cold plunge room on its own, or combine it with an infrared sauna in one of our contrast therapy suites in Dubai Media City.

    How cold is it?

    Our plunges are kept between 3.5 and 8°C. The water is cleaned around the clock: ozone is injected during filtration 24/7, and we treat it daily with spa-grade, non-toxic sanitisers.

    Your first cold plunge

    Don’t worry – there are no heroes here. Start with 30 seconds to a minute and build up over your visits. If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, please check with your doctor first. Shivering afterwards is normal; let your body warm up naturally, or take a room-temperature shower.

    Sessions

    Cold plunge room bookings are 15 or 30 minutes, which gives you time to shower before and after. Showering before your plunge is required, and we provide a towel. Bringing a friend? The room has a one-person barrel so you can take turns (30-minute sessions only).

    Why cold?

    • Mood and focus – cold water triggers a big release of dopamine and norepinephrine, the brain’s natural energy and focus boosters.
    • Recovery – cold immersion helps soothe sore, tired muscles.
    • Stress resilience – short, controlled exposure to cold trains your body to stay calm under stress.
    • Sleep – a morning plunge helps set your body clock for the day.

    Benefits

    Cold plunges are great for your brain. They reduce anxiety, improve mood, boost energy levels and immunity, and increase longevity. Studies show just a few minutes in cold water can spike dopamine by 250%, giving you a natural high.

    Cold exposure also increases norepinephrine, enhancing focus, attention, vigilance, and mood. This can lead to feelings of calm, happiness, and well-being, helping to mitigate depression and anxiety symptoms.

    Cold plunges and ice baths offer a unique fat-burning solution beyond the gym. Cold exposure activates brown fat, a special type that burns calories to generate heat, potentially helping you shed fat faster. Studies show that cold water immersion significantly boosts metabolic rate, with regular winter swimmers burning up to 1,000 kcal/24 hr more than their counterparts, even at rest in warm environments.

    A 2023 study published in Biology revealed that a five-minute cold plunge left participants feeling more active, alert, attentive, proud, and inspired, while reducing feelings of distress and nervousness.

    Cold stimulation also activates the vagus nerve, which regulates the nervous system during stress, suggesting ice baths and cold plunges could offer similar benefits.

    Cold plunging can enhance sleep due to releasing melatonin and by aligning your circadian rhythm. Factors like sunlight and the sympathetic nervous system calibrate this daily rhythm. Morning cold plunges wake your body, activate the sympathetic nervous system, and synchronize your circadian rhythm’s “start” phase.

    Regular cold water exposure reduces inflammation and improves blood flow. Your blood vessels contract in the cold, then gradually dilate, enhancing circulation and lymphatic function. This process, known as the ‘dive effect,’ puts your body into survival mode to maintain core temperature. According to Wim Hof, this boosts cardiovascular circulation, benefiting the heart, supporting mental health, and providing a high energy level.

    Cold plunging helps your body to recover faster. Especially for athletes, cold water immersion is a relief to sore and burning muscles.

    Cold water plunging boosts your immune system by stimulating leukocytes (white blood cells) that fight off infections. It also activates the lymphatic system, contracting it and pushing fluid through lymph nodes for a natural immune boost.
