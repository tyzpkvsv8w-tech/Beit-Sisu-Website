## Frequently asked questions

**How cold does it get?**
With the chiller connected, the water stays cold throughout your rental. Without power, we can supply ice to bring the temperature down. Message us and we'll recommend the best option for your space.

**Can it go outdoors?**
Yes, the ice baths work indoors and outdoors.

**Do you deliver and set it up?**
Yes. We deliver the ice bath, set it up for you and collect it at the end of your rental.

**Do you deliver outside Dubai?**
We cover Dubai and the surrounding areas as standard. Other emirates are possible on request.

**How many people can use it?**
Each ice bath is designed for one person at a time. For events and teams, ask us about renting more than one.

**Is cold plunging safe?**
For most healthy adults, short plunges are safe. Start with 30 seconds to a minute, and check with your doctor first if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, are pregnant or have any other medical condition. Read our [beginner's guide to ice baths](/how-cold-is-an-ice-bath/).

**How do I book?**
Message us on WhatsApp with your dates, location and whether you have power available, and we'll take it from there.
