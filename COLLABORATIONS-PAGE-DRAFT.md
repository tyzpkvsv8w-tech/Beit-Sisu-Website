## Partner with Beit Sisu

From team wellness days to community events, we love working with people who care about recovery as much as we do. Beit Sisu is a private contrast therapy studio inside the Arjaan by Rotana Hotel in Dubai Media City, with infrared saunas, cold plunges and red light therapy in fully private suites.

## Ways we work together

- **Corporate wellness:** recovery sessions for your team at our studio, or a wellness perk for your staff.
- **Clubs and communities:** running clubs, golf societies, sports teams and social groups. We create a dedicated discount code for your members.
- **Events and activations:** bring the cold plunge to you with our [portable ice bath rental](/ice-bath-rental-dubai/). Delivered, set up and collected, indoor or outdoor.
- **Brands and creators:** product launches, content shoots and collaborations in a calm, private space.

## Why Beit Sisu?

- Completely private suites, so no shared rooms or crowds
- Central location in Dubai Media City, minutes from Dubai Marina, JBR and Palm Jumeirah, with free valet parking
- A team that does contrast therapy every day, and can guide first-timers
